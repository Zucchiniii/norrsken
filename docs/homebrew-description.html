Norrsken: NordVPN for rooted LG TVs

An unofficial NordVPN client that sends the whole TV through NordLynx (NordVPN's WireGuard). That covers Netflix, YouTube, the browser and every other app. Your home network stays local, so casting and the ThinQ app keep working.

Features

  • Fastest server, every NordVPN country, and cities
  • Link from your phone: scan a QR code and paste your access token
  • DNS through NordVPN, IPv6 blocked while connected, optional kill switch
  • Threat Protection Lite (blocks ad, tracker and malware domains)
  • Connect when the TV starts, automatic server failover, self-repairing routes

You need

  • A rooted TV (Homebrew Channel shows Root status: ok)
  • A NordVPN subscription and an access token. In Nord Account, open NordVPN and scroll down to Advanced settings, below Manual configuration.
